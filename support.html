單頁文件和多頁文件有什麼不同？

單頁文件：每拍一張就自動存成一份文件。多頁文件：連續拍很多頁，頁面會先放在「工作空間」，按「完成」後可以匯出 PDF、Word，或儲存到文件。

文件存在哪裡？

只存在你的 iPhone 上這個 App 裡，不會上傳。換手機前請先把需要的文件匯出。

拍出來不夠清楚怎麼辦？

讓文件平放、光線充足，等畫面上的藍框穩定再拍。拍完可以按「調整」拖曳四個角、旋轉或換濾鏡。

怎麼恢復或取消訂閱？

恢復：App 內「設定 → 恢復購買」。取消：iPhone「設定 → 你的名字 → 訂閱」。

Single Page or Multi-Page?

Single Page saves every photo as its own document. Multi-Page keeps shooting and collects the pages in Workspaces; tap Finish to export a PDF or Word file, or save it to Documents.

Where are my documents?

Only in this app on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded, so export what you need before changing phones.

The scan is not sharp enough

Lay the page flat in good light and wait for the blue outline to settle. Afterwards tap Adjust to move the corners, rotate or change the filter.

Restore or cancel a subscription

Restore: Settings → Restore Purchases in the app. Cancel: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
